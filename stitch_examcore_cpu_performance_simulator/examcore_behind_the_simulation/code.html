01 // INVESTIGATION DIAGNOSTIC CASE STUDY

The Synchronous Exam Submission Convoy

During an online examination, hundreds of students load questions, save answers, and submit responses simultaneously when the examination timer lapses. In traditional First-Come First-Served architectures, large payload submissions (such as cryptographic answer hashing) monopolize CPU cores, causing catastrophic convoy blocking where lightweight auto-saves and heartbeat pings experience severe timeout freezes.
